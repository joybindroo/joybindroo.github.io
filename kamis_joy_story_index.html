The KAMIS Register

Eight years of sent and received mail, read back as one story: how a rebuilt data system for tracking Kala-azar (visceral leishmaniasis) across Bihar, Jharkhand, and beyond grew from a buggy pilot into the operational backbone of a national elimination programme — and how Joy Bindroo's role grew with it, from data-entry troubleshooter to the person WHO and NVBDCP call when the system needs to talk to the rest of the government.

Compiled from CARE India's KAMIS-related Sent Items and Inbox exports (Jul 2023 snapshot). Chapters are narrative syntheses of email headers and bodies mentioning KAMIS each year — names, dates and events are drawn directly from that correspondence, supplemented by targeted follow-up searches (VLDRS/Kaszona, DDMS/LSTM, LSHTM's SPEAK India collaboration, the Village Mapping Exercise, and KAMIS's mobile-app history) and by direct clarifications from Joy Bindroo on design roles and the 2016 DHIS2 comparison. KAMIS = Kala-azar Management Information System.
